Methodology and limitations

Why this is a careful estimate rather than an exact bank total

How the estimate was built

Order confirmations were separated from newsletters, promotions, review requests, unsuccessful orders and cancellations. Receipts from different years were sampled to avoid applying modern prices retrospectively. Basket composition was adjusted because some pizza-restaurant orders contained desserts or sides only, while some larger orders likely contained multiple pizzas.

Known uncertainty

Cash orders, telephone orders, deleted messages and receipts sent to other accounts are not visible. A small number of duplicate, refunded or replacement confirmations may remain. Figures are nominal pounds paid at the time and are not inflation-adjusted.

Earliest unambiguous Gmail evidence: a Just Eat pizza order dated 6 July 2009. No confirmed pizza receipts were found for 2005–2008, which may reflect offline ordering, another email account or missing messages.
